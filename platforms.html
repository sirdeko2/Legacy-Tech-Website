VoteGuard
Election Monitoring & War Room Analytics Platform

VoteGuard is a real-time election monitoring, analytics, and war room platform built specifically for the Liberian electoral context. It aggregates live data from field observers, polling station reports, results tallies, and social media monitoring into a single unified command center — serving five distinct stakeholders: the National Elections Commission, political parties, international observer missions, media organisations, and civil society NGOs.

The platform is built around one core principle: elections are won or lost in real time. VoteGuard moves election integrity from after-the-fact documentation to real-time detection and response. An early demo is now live.
